# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Settings cards fit their contents
Settings SHALL show its cards in two independent vertical columns, with each card sized to its own contents. ProRewards sign-in and Updates SHALL stack on the left; Appearance and Logs SHALL stack on the right.

#### Scenario: Shorter cards in each row
- **WHEN** the person opens Settings
- **THEN** the Appearance card is shorter than ProRewards sign-in, and the Logs card is shorter than Updates
- **AND** Logs appears directly below Appearance with the normal card gap
