# Proposal

## Why

Review and Enter claims show several nested scrollbars. Their appearance shifts nearby content and makes the screens look crowded.

## What Changes

- Hide scrollbar tracks on the Review and Enter claims app screens.
- Keep page, table, side sheet, activity, and invoice list scrolling functional.
- Leave other screens and the separate ProRewards webpage unchanged.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-ui`: Scrolling presentation on Review and Enter claims.
